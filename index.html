    Largest majors, Fall 2025

      About the data. Counts are fall-semester headcount by major as supplied by UNL Institutional Research for Fall 2016 through Fall 2025 and include every degree level listed under that major name. Students are counted once for each major they declare, so a double major appears in both. Programs are matched by the name used in each year’s file; when a program was renamed, split or retired, the line starts or stops (or has a gap) rather than being merged with a similar program. The college shown is the one listed in the latest year the major appears; graduate programs are listed under their Grad Studies college.
