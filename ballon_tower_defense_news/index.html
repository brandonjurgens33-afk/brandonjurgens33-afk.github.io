Wrecking Ball Defense

Two University of Nebraska–Lincoln landmarks are scheduled for demolition and rebuilding. In this game, you hold off the wrecking balls. Read the real stories below, then pick a map and defend it.

Map 1 · Memorial Stadium

‘Big Red Rebuilt’: Memorial Stadium to get a $600 million renovation

Nebraska Athletics plans to tear down and rebuild parts of the 100-year-old home of Husker football.

  • South Stadium and the West Stadium seating bowl would be demolished, and South Stadium rebuilt with a large video board.
  • Capacity drops from about 85,000 to 80,000, with 7,000 seats for students and the band.
  • Work is set to start after the 2026 season and finish by the 2028 season.
  • Paid for with $250 million in donations and $350 million in private bonds. No tuition or tax dollars.
The Daily Nebraskan · Justin Diep and Logan Moseley · April 17, 2026

Map 2 · Selleck Dining Hall

Selleck Quadrangle to be demolished and rebuilt in a $386 million project

The NU Board of Regents approved replacing the 1950s-era Selleck Quad with a new home for first-year students on City Campus.

  • Selleck, the old Health Center, and Seaton, Benton and Fairfield halls will come down.
  • In their place: a 1,700-bed residence hall plus a new dining center and retail space.
  • Demolition is expected in 2028. Phase one (800 beds and the new dining hall) opens in 2030, and phase two (900 beds) in 2032.
  • The Selleck dining hall stays open during construction. No tax or tuition dollars are used.
The Daily Nebraskan · Justin Diep · June 23, 2026

How to play

Goal

Keep the wrecking balls from reaching the building. Survive all 40 rounds to save the landmark. Every ball that gets through takes away lives, and if your lives hit zero, the building is demolished.

Rules

  1. Pick a tower, then click beside the road to place it. You can't build on the road, buildings or trees.
  2. Each hit knocks a ball down one layer: pink → gold → green → blue → rusty → gone.
  3. A ball that gets through costs one life for every layer it has left.
  4. You earn cash for every layer you pop and for every round you finish. Spend it on new towers and upgrades.
  5. Each tower has two upgrade paths with three tiers. Only one path can reach tier 3. Selling a tower returns 70% of what you spent.
  6. Concrete balls block sharp and cold attacks. Use hay bales, mortar shells, footballs, lightning or upgrades.
  7. Mega balls take 10 hits, and Demolition Crane bosses roll in on rounds 20, 25, 30, 35 and 40.
Controls
  • Click a tower button, then click the map. Hold Shift to place several.
  • Click a placed tower to upgrade it, change its targeting or sell it.
  • Space starts the next round, F changes speed (1×, 2×, 3×), 1–9, 0 pick towers, Esc cancels and Delete sells.

This is a fan-made game inspired by Daily Nebraskan reporting. It is not affiliated with The Daily Nebraskan or the University of Nebraska.
